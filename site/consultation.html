Let’s work out whether it fits

Amlak is built narrowly and on purpose, which means it suits some portfolios very well and others not at all. So this is a conversation rather than a signup: tell us what you’re running, and we’ll tell you honestly whether this is the right tool for it. That answer is useful to you either way, and it costs nothing.

  • Email or a short call — your choice. Nobody is going to ring you because you filled in a form.
  • Nothing to buy. No card, no contract, no price yet. This is not a sales call wearing a different name.
  • We will tell you if it doesn’t fit. Residential, hundreds of units, or a portal for your tenants — that is not this product, and you’ll hear that from us rather than find it out in month three.
  • You are talking to the person who built it. Not a rep working from a script about a product they have never used.
