1. What Amlak is

Software for managing commercial property leases: reading them, working out what tenants owe under them, tracking what has been paid, and producing the documents that go with all of that.

4. Figures, documents, and where judgement stays

Amlak is a tool, not an adviser. Nothing it produces is accounting, tax, or legal advice. It computes from what you and your documents tell it. The reconciliation it builds, the invoice it drafts, the share it calculates — you are the one issuing those, and you are responsible for them.

Check what the AI read. When Amlak extracts terms from a lease it shows each one next to the clause it came from precisely so you can verify it. That is not decoration. A document can be ambiguous, badly scanned, or contradicted by an amendment, and a term Amlak could not find is marked as not found rather than guessed. Confirming that the extraction matches your lease is your job, not ours.

Electronic signatures. Amlak produces signed documents with an audit trail intended to satisfy the usual requirements for electronic signatures. Whether a particular document is validly executed — and whether it says what you want it to say — is between you, the other party, and your lawyer.
