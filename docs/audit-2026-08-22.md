### 1. ✔ ↩ Undo after "Save flat CAM" writes $0, not the previous figure — and rebuilds every tenant's bill at zero CAM

`HIGH` · **Financials** · fails question *5 - after: what happened (the undo reports one thing and does another)*

**Evidence.** src/components/CamSection.js:412 calls `saveFlat.mutate()` with NO argument, so the `prevCam` react-query hands to `onSuccess: (_data, prevCam)` (CamSection.js:148) is `undefined`. ### 2. The service-contract / management-fee CAM sync never runs on the Financials page — a second observer on the same query key wins the fetch

`HIGH` · **Financials** · fails question *5 - after: what happened (the contract's cost never reaches the CAM it is said to feed)*

**What a client sees.** Two things a client sees. (1) A management fee entered as "% of rent" stops matching its own label: after any rent change the CAM row prints "5% of $150,000.00 base rent" (computed live) next to a stored "$7,200.00" (5% of the old $144,000) — an arithmetic contradiction inside one row, on screen, forever. (2) A multi-year service contract never gets its CAM line when a new fiscal year is opened, and a dated fee step never re-prices the existing line: the Contracts tab prints "CAM 2027: $12,360" while the Financials CAM section shows $12,000 or nothing at all — two screens quoting different annual costs for one contract, with the tenants' CAM shares and the year's invoices following the lower one.

**Evidence.** src/components/CamSection.js:30-45 wraps `syncContractCamItems` + `syncRentPctCamItems` inside the `['camLineItems', propId, year]` queryFn, which is the mechanism the codebase relies on ("opening any fiscal year keeps its contract costs and management fee current"; api.js:3597 "viewing the year self-heals it"; CLAUDE.md §1 "CamSection triggers it on every year open"). But src/lib/useRecoverability.js:38-41 registers an observer on the SAME key with a plain `listCamLineItems` queryFn, and it mounts FIRST on this page — WhatStayedStrip at src/pages/PropertyFinancialsPage.js:243 vs CamSection at :315. React Query keeps only the queryFn of the observer that moves the query out of `idle`. Verified empirically: mounting CamSection alone → syncContractCamItems 1, syncRentPctCamItems 1; mounting the real PropertyFinancialsPage (with the CAM add-form on screen, i.e. CamSection genuinely mounted) → 0 and 0. `grep -rn syncContractCamItems src` shows the only other caller is `carryContractChange` (src/lib/api.js:2380), which runs on a contract create/edit/delete and covers only years that already have an `expense_records` row plus the current one (api.js:2372-2378) — so nothing else creates a new fiscal year's contract line, and nothing else applies a `contract_escalations` step (which is derived, never swept).

**Fix.** Take the writes out of the read. Run the two syncs from an effect in CamSection (or from PropertyFinancialsPage) and invalidate `['camLineItems', propId, year]` when either reports `changed`, instead of hiding them in a queryFn that a co-observer can pre-empt. Minimum viable alternative: give the CamSection read its own key (e.g. `['camLineItemsSynced', propId, year]`) so it can never share a query with useRecoverability. Either way, no query on this page should carry a queryFn that writes while a second observer shares its key.

**Caveat.** Verified in jsdom against the demo mock; the ordering rule is a React Query invariant rather than a jsdom artefact, but a live click-through is cheap confirmation. Note also that a property whose contracts get edited each year will look correct, because `carryContractChange` covers that path — the gap is the passive "open the year and it self-heals" path.

### 5. The ✉ Statement letter prints two contradictory "difference" figures and a balance matching neither, whenever a CAM & tax correction was posted during the year

`HIGH` · **Reconciliation** · fails question *5 - after: what happened, and 9 - is the failure legible (here: an arithmetic statement sent to a tenant that does not add up)*

**Evidence.** src/lib/reconciliation.js:253 folds `camTaxAdjust` into `estCamTax`, so `fig.diff` (:266) carries it. src/lib/api.js:5222-5228 stores `est_cam: fig.est.cam` / `est_tax: fig.est.tax` — the PRE-adjustment figures — beside `diff: fig.diff`, the post-adjustment one; nothing on cam_reconciliations records the adjustment. src/lib/api.js:5331-5337 `draftCamReconciliationEmail` rebuilds the lines from those stored columns. src/lib/emailTemplates.js:411-419 computes each line's `d` locally as `actual − est` (so the line reads +$300) while :438 prints the TOTAL row with the passed-in `diff` (−$100). Meanwhile the invoice's own notes (api.js:5205-5209) use `fig.lines[].est`, which DOES include the correction — so the invoice and the letter quote different "billed" figures for the same year.

**Fix.** Store the correction on the record (add a `cam_tax_adjust` column to cam_reconciliations, or fold it into `est_cam` at insert time the way `fig.lines` does) and give the letter the same memo line the workbook got on 2026-08-16: `of which corrections billed during the year (N posted) …`.
